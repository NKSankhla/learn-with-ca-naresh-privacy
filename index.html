Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Learn With CA Naresh (the “App”), developed by CA Naresh Kumar Sankhla (“we”, “us” or “our”), handles information when you use the App.

The App provides educational content and learning tools for commerce and CA Foundation students.

1. Information stored on your device

The App may store learning information on your device so that features can work, such as bookmarks, quiz attempts and answers, scores, learning progress, history and preferences. Based on the current product design, this information is intended to remain on your device and is not intended to be sent to the developer’s servers.

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in to use its learning features.

2. Information we collect or receive

The intended current design does not include advertising, push notifications, account registration, or a server-based progress-sync feature. We do not intentionally collect your learning progress through a developer-operated account or sync service.

These statements describe the intended app design and must be kept accurate as the App changes. If a future version adds analytics, crash reporting, online accounts, cloud sync, advertising, payments, or other services that process information, this Policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

3. Third-party services and links

The App uses software libraries to display content and provide device features. A library’s presence does not by itself mean that personal information is sent to its provider. We will disclose any third-party service that collects or receives information through the App if such a service is used.

If you choose to open an external website, email link, phone link, or another app, that service may process information under its own privacy policy. Please review the relevant service’s terms and privacy information.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. The App does not currently provide a learning-progress export or import feature. If a future feature allows you to share information, the App will explain what is shared and how the sharing action works.

5. Data retention and deletion

Learning information stored locally remains on your device until it is removed by the App, overwritten, or deleted through device settings. Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may delete locally stored information. We do not currently provide a cloud account from which learning progress can be retrieved or deleted.

6. Security

We aim to limit information handling to what is needed to provide the App’s educational features. Information stored on your device is subject to the security of your device and operating system. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children and students

The App is intended for commerce and CA Foundation learners generally. It is not specifically designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a service connected to the App, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the App’s features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date.
